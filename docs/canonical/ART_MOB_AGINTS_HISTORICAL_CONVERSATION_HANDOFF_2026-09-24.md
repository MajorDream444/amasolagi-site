## 1. Executive summary
AMA Solutions is an agentic agency and delivery business. Art Mob AGINTS supplies specialist digital workers, organized into Mobs that resemble independently marketable companies but collaborate on one shared global campus. AMA sells outcomes: research, growth, sales enablement, customer experience, content, data intelligence, operations automation, products, and specialist support. Each AGINT combines an identity/persona with bounded skills, tools, context, playbooks, handoffs, and human oversight; clients can hire an AGINT or a full Mob. The fundamental differentiator proposed here is pairing emerging technical operators with experienced industry veterans so the system preserves hard-earned wisdom while modernizing workflows.

**HAMAL = Hanzo + AMA (Art Mob AGINTS / AMA Solutions) + Lux.** Hanzo supplies AI infrastructure, AMA orchestrates and delivers work, and Lux is the proposed blockchain/provenance/identity layer wherever contractually and technically integrated. These are distinct entities/capabilities, not automatically one owned balance sheet. **MAIM = Major Artificial Intelligence Mindset** is the education and operator-development pathway. Do not use inconsistent legacy spelling for MAIM. 

**Homepage positioning:** "We give you AGINTS and Mobs that get the work done." Supporting idea: AI workers + skilled human reinforcement + accountable playbooks produce measurable business outcomes. Describe represented humans and AI transparently; do not imply AI is a licensed human professional or disguise an AI interaction.

## 2. Founder doctrine and reason for the model
Major Dream Williams frames AMA as capacity transfer, not merely selling chatbots. Businesses have senior people with decades of industry knowledge and operational pain; young engineers and no-code creators have new technical tools but may lack domain context. An adaptive AGINT sits between them, converting approved wisdom into useful workflows, supported by emerging operators who earn from delivered value. A broader mission is education-to-work: recruit and train operators, including talent in Southeast Asia and Caribbean communities, provide paid assignments, and eventually enable entrepreneurship and white-label products. Early discussion mentioned outreach to single mothers and talent underserved by conventional technology pipelines; treat this as an inclusion goal, not a claim of current hiring outcomes.

Product architecture envisioned: named personalities and specialization -> organizational Mobs -> structured intake -> scoped assignments -> tool execution and human review -> outcome measurement -> contextual improvement -> reusable playbooks -> compliant provenance records for approved contributions and versions. Improvements are not automatic model-weight training. Customer confidential data is never pooled into reusable agents without explicit consent, rights, segregation, and governance.

## 11. Sales journey and website routes
1. **Arrival**: founder narrative / campaign / article / interactive campus.
2. **Routing**: "What outcome are you trying to achieve?" e.g., more leads, support customers, reduce admin burden, launch a product, manage athlete careers, protect brand.
3. **Mob match**: propose lead Mob + collaborative specialist Mobs and explain why.
4. **Basic intake**: full name, company, email, phone (optional depending jurisdiction), website, operating country, industry, role, team size, referral source.
5. **Qualified discovery**: goals, pain points, current tools, process volumes, privacy/compliance needs, success metrics, timeline, budget/authority/owner.
6. **Assessment**: establish process baseline, access permissions, feasibility, human review obligations.
7. **Scope + pilot**: specific deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, approval owner, measurement method and price.
8. **Delivery**: orchestrate Mobs, control tool permissions, record auditable events; review output with human.
9. **Evidence and handoff**: baseline-versus-result report, dashboards, client training, signoff, ongoing plan.
10. **Expansion**: renewal, white-label only where rights allow, cross-Mob engagement, trained client staff or MAIM pathway.

Intake must collect *minimum necessary information*, use role-based access, disclose AI assistance and avoid requesting confidential files before signed engagement/data handling terms.
