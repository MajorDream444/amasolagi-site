## Reconciliation and editorial usage

- Early chat: 10 groups × 14 = 140, not 144. Later two Mobs were added, without validated full rosters. Newer architecture plans 12 × 12 specialists = 144.
- Earlier persona names changed across generations. All names here are historical creative examples pending registry authority.
- Content titles, team colors, tools and architecture in this annex are proposals unless backed by deployment evidence.
- Never put confidential client data or health information on a public blockchain.
- Route visitors to outcomes and an intake pathway. Do not claim a service is already delivered merely because the campus presents a fictional AGINT.
