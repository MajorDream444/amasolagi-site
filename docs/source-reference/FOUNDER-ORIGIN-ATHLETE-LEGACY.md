# Founder Origin and Athlete Legacy — Historical Context

**Use:** internal background for AMA Solutions website and founder storytelling. This document preserves the earlier athlete-centered vision while distinguishing it from AMA’s current broader company and HAMAL direction. It is not approved final website copy.

## Origin of the work

Major Dream Williams’s experience as a former athlete and his observation of changes in amateur and professional sports helped shape AMA’s early focus: athletes should be equipped to manage their careers as businesses, understand the value of their Name, Image, and Likeness (NIL), and build financial and cultural legacies beyond active competition.

The historical narrative also connects this mission to Major’s late brother, Corey “Homicide” Williams, described in the source material as a beloved basketball player. Any public treatment should be reviewed by Major for accuracy, tone, and family privacy.

AMA’s founding concept extended beyond sports. It included using technology and education to widen access to the digital economy, with particular concern for underserved communities, single mothers, and families in the Caribbean and Southeast Asia. Low-code/no-code education, financial literacy, and entrepreneurship were proposed pathways.

## Historical product and program concepts

The earlier AMA materials proposed:

- Athlete NIL and career-management support
- Digital collectibles and tokenized career milestones
- Blockchain-based records and smart-contract revenue sharing
- Performance, audience, and market analytics
- Financial literacy and blockchain education
- An AI career companion
- Fan participation and community engagement
- Youth sports, scholarships, and education workshops
- Low-code/no-code learning and digital-economy pathways for single mothers and underserved communities
