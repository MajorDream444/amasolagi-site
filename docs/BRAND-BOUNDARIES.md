# Brand Boundaries: AMA Solutions, Major Dream Williams, and MAIM

**Purpose:** keep AMA Solutions’ public website focused on the corporation while preserving clear routes to the founder’s personal and education work.

## AMA Solutions website

AMA Solutions is the corporate website. Its primary job is to explain:

- What AMA Solutions Corp does
- How HAMAL coordinates the system
- What the 12 Art Mob teams are designed to deliver
- What Foundry OS is and its current stage
- How strategic partners, clients, and potential investors can engage with AMA

AMA website copy should focus on corporate capabilities, initiatives, delivery model, verified work, and company direction. It should not become Major’s personal doctrine or a full archive of his life and projects.

## Major Dream Williams personal website

The personal website at [majordreamwilliams.com](https://majordreamwilliams.com) is the home for Major’s broader public identity and body of work, including:

- Personal journey, philosophy, and doctrine
- Books and writing
- Speaking, media, and personal projects
- A founder narrative that spans multiple ventures
- Links to AMA Solutions, MAIM, and other initiatives

Personal website: [majordreamwilliams.com](https://majordreamwilliams.com). The domain is active; site deployment and DNS routing are not yet verified.

## Major AI Mindset (MAIM)

MAIM is the education-focused platform at [majoraimindset.com](https://majoraimindset.com) for helping people build confidence, literacy, and practical skill with AI. Keep its curriculum, learning resources, community, and educational calls to action on the MAIM site.

MAIM’s domain is [majoraimindset.com](https://majoraimindset.com). The domain is active; site deployment and DNS routing are not yet verified. The AMA site may identify MAIM as a related initiative in a small “Ecosystem” or “Related work” area if useful, but should route education visitors to MAIM rather than reproduce its content.

## Founder presence on AMA

A short founder section on AMA is appropriate when it helps visitors understand why the corporation exists and who leads it. Keep it concise and company-relevant:

- Major’s role at AMA
- The relevant connection between his experience and AMA’s purpose
- A link to the personal site once its domain is confirmed

Do not place the full personal biography, personal doctrine, book catalog, MAIM curriculum, or unrelated founder projects on the AMA website.

## Content routing test

Before adding founder or initiative content to the AMA site, ask:

1. Does this help a visitor understand AMA as a company?
2. Does it support a client, strategic partner, or investor decision about AMA?
3. Is this material company-owned or explicitly approved for AMA?

If not, route it to Major’s personal website or MAIM.
