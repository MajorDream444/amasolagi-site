# AMA Solutions Website — Project Brief

**Owner:** Major Dream Williams / AMA Solutions Corp  
**Audience:** prospective strategic partners, investors, and clients  
**Purpose:** create one clear public home for AMA Solutions and the work it is building.

## Positioning

AMA Solutions is an agentic venture-building and delivery company. It brings people, specialized agent teams, technical capabilities, and operating playbooks together to turn ambitious ideas into scoped, reviewable work.

Suggested working line:

> We build systems that turn human potential into lasting ownership.

Treat this as draft website copy pending owner approval.

## The operating model

### AMA Solutions Corp
The corporation is the public company, client relationship owner, contracting entity when a contract is in scope, and intended investment vehicle. AMA is the front door for commercial engagements and strategic relationships.

### HAMAL
HAMAL is the top coordinating Mob and intelligence layer across Hanzo, AMA, and Lux. It maintains broad system context, develops playbooks and orchestration standards, and helps the 12 delivery Mobs share useful learning. HAMAL enables the delivery network; clients normally engage the relevant Art Mob team for defined work.

### The 12 Art Mob teams
The 12 Mobs are the client-facing delivery units. The design envisions 144 Art Mob AGInts distributed across 12 Mobs. A client can engage a full Mob or a suitable portion of one, depending on the scope. Show the campus as a navigable story and capability map, while clearly distinguishing envisioned organization from verified deployments.

### Foundry OS
Foundry OS is in development as a modular operating system for coordinating agent-enabled work, with a longer-term intention to make parts of the system operational and licensable. Do not describe it as a generally available product until that status is verified.

### Hanzo and Lux
Hanzo contributes AI and technical capabilities; Lux contributes sovereignty, provenance, or related infrastructure where applicable. HAMAL connects these capabilities to AMA’s delivery architecture. Describe concrete implementations only when confirmed for the specific project.

## Primary audiences and actions

1. **Strategic partners:** understand the architecture and open a conversation.
2. **Investors:** understand AMA as the company they may invest in and request an investment conversation. Do not publish securities terms or imply an offering is open unless separately approved.
3. **Clients:** identify the problem-solving capabilities and contact AMA to scope a paid engagement.

## Tone and personality

Confident, imaginative, human, and specific. Let the founder’s perspective and the campus concept bring personality. Keep the company, offer, and next step understandable to people who do not know the internal system names.

## Truth and stage labels

Use clear labels such as **Vision**, **In development**, **Prototype**, and **Verified live**. Verify status with the owner before publishing an operational claim. The 12-Mob catalog describes intended service domains; it does not by itself verify that every agent, tool, service, integration, or price is live.

## Do not publish without confirmation

- Exact domain and email addresses
- Current offers, prices, and booking availability
- Investor terms, SAFE terms, or public solicitation language
- Client logos, results, testimonials, or case studies
- Partner commitments or claims that Hanzo or Lux is deployed on a specific engagement
- Claims that all 144 AGInts are active or autonomous
- Claims that Foundry OS is production-ready or available to license today
