## The AMA analysis method
**Map:** define the intended outcome, current process, people, inputs, baseline and where work appears stuck. Examine reach, acquisition, conversion and expansion, plus delivery, quality and capacity. Do not infer the constraint from the lowest percentage alone.

**Poke:** ask whether that constraint explains the outcome. Seek contrary evidence, competing causes, permissions and missing facts. Distinguish client assertions, independently checked facts, hypotheses and recommendations. If no baseline exists, the next step may be measurement rather than automation.

**Scale:** select the smallest useful intervention. First consider simplification, clearer responsibilities or better information; automation is an option. Break the intended outcome into actionable tasks and choose which need human judgment, software or digital workers. Baseline the expected effort and define success before expansion.

**Pressure-proof:** test repeated outcomes, failure cases, human review, ownership and handover. Compare results against a baseline and record what failed. Promote a reusable playbook only after review. A single strong demo is not a deployed reliable system. When one constraint moves, reassess the next one.

## How AMA reaches a conclusion
Every paid analysis records: the decision question; source IDs and dates; observed facts; material contradictions; unknowns; alternative explanations; confidence rationale; recommended next step; responsible owner; success measure; and a review date or invalidation trigger. Evidence supports claims. Popularity, repetition or fluent AI prose is not independent confirmation. Do not use numerical confidence without a calibrated method.

Recommendations may be: proceed with a scoped deliverable, gather evidence, run a small test, bring in a qualified specialist, or decline unsuitable work. Never route a visitor to an irrelevant product merely to generate a sale.

## Relationship path
Free orientation prepares the buyer. A scoped product or $350 session addresses one need. The $1,250 workshop develops a deeper workflow design. The buyer can implement the deliverable themselves, use their engineer, or request a separate AMA implementation proposal. Any delivery/maintenance proposal must specify owner, milestones, dependencies, recurring charges if any, acceptance tests and support. No implementation quote is invented by the website agent.

## Launch selection
Feature Walk In Ready, Make It Make Sense Kit, Customer Question Kit and AI Choice Session. Keep Find Your Opening and Idea to MVP visible as limited pilot routes. Explain It Monthly follows a proven single kit and recurring topic backlog. The remaining catalog remains discoverable with honest availability labels. A catalog entry does not imply a live purchase path.

## Learning and coherence
Coherence means consistent schemas, source attribution and reviewed reusable methods. It does not mean shared access to every customer's private material. Improve playbooks through consented, minimized examples and human-approved evaluations. Do not automatically train on conversations or cross-pollinate confidential client memory.
